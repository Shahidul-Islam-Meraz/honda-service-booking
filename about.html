Honda Bangladesh Service Booking

What this service does

Honda Bangladesh Service Booking lets Honda customers in Bangladesh book a service appointment with an authorised Honda dealer. The customer picks a dealer, a service type, a date and a time slot. The dealer then confirms, cancels or completes the appointment from a dealer dashboard.

Book a service appointment

Email notifications

Customers and dealers receive email notifications about each booking: a new booking alert for the dealer, and a confirmation, cancellation or completion notice for the customer.

Why dealers connect Gmail

A dealer can connect their own Gmail account so that booking emails to their customers are sent from the dealer's own address. We ask for one Google permission only: send email on the dealer's behalf. We do not read, search, change or delete any email in the dealer's mailbox.

Dealers can disconnect at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
