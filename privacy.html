Honda Bangladesh Service Booking

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Back to home page

This policy explains what information Honda Bangladesh Service Booking ("the service", operated by Bangladesh Honda Private Limited) collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Information we collect from customers

When you book a service appointment we collect your name, mobile number, optional email address, optional chassis number, the dealer you chose, the service type, the appointment date and time, and any remarks you enter. We use this only to arrange and manage your appointment and to send you booking notifications.

Information we receive from dealers

Dealers sign in to a dashboard with a dealer code and PIN. A dealer may also connect their own Gmail account to the service.

How we use Google user data

When a dealer connects Gmail, we request one Google permission only: the ability to send email on the dealer's behalf (the gmail.send scope). We use it solely to send booking notification emails: new booking alerts, and confirmation, cancellation and completion notices to customers.

We do not read, search, list, change or delete any email in the dealer's mailbox. We do not access contacts or any other Google data.

To send emails without asking the dealer to sign in each time, we store a secure access token for the dealer's connection. It is stored in our database, is used only to send these notification emails, and is never shown to other users.

The service's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share Google user data with third parties. Booking details are shared only with the dealer you chose, and with the infrastructure providers that host the service (database and email delivery) so it can operate.

Retention and deletion

Booking records are kept for as long as needed to run the service and for record-keeping. A dealer can disconnect Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can ask us to delete the stored connection. Customers can ask us to correct or delete their booking information.

Security

Access to booking data is restricted. Dealers can see only their own bookings, and stored credentials are not exposed to the public website.
